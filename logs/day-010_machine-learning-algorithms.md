5. MAE (Mean Absolute Error)
   - Measures the average absolute difference between actual and predicted values.
   - Lower MAE means better model performance.

6. MSE (Mean Squared Error)
   - Measures the average squared difference between actual and predicted values.
   - Penalizes large errors more strongly than MAE.
   - Lower MSE means better performance.

7. RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error)
   - Square root of MSE.
   - RMSE is in the same unit as the target variable.
   - Lower RMSE means better model performance.

8. R2 Score (R-Squared)
   - Measures how well the model explains the variation in the target variable.
   - Higher R2 generally indicates a better fit.
   - R2 = 1 means the model explains all observed variation perfectly.
